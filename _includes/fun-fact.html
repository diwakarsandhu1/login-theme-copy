  • The word "salary" comes from the Latin "salarium," believed to be linked to payments Roman soldiers received for buying salt.
  • The New York Stock Exchange started in 1792 when 24 stockbrokers signed the Buttonwood Agreement under a buttonwood tree on Wall Street.
  • Compound interest was called "the eighth wonder of the world" by Albert Einstein for its power to grow wealth over time.
  • The first paper money was used in China during the Tang Dynasty, over a thousand years before it appeared in Europe.
  • The Dutch East India Company, founded in 1602, is considered the first company to issue publicly traded stock.
  • The U.S. penny costs more than one cent to produce, meaning the government loses money every time it mints one.
  • The term "bull market" is thought to come from how a bull attacks by thrusting its horns upward, symbolizing rising prices.
  • The Monopoly board game was originally designed in 1903 to teach players about the dangers of wealth concentration and monopolies.
  • Warren Buffett bought his first stock at age 11 and has said he wishes he had started even earlier.
  • The world's first credit card, the Diners Club Card, was created in 1950 after a businessman forgot his wallet at dinner.
